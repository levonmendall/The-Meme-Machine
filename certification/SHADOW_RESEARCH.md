# Separate four-lane shadow research registration

This revision is a preregistration and read-only scoring path. It does not change
the code running in workflow 35483374004 (integration
ebd03f5780eee46cc59f0338f48830e475480d78), strategy authority, eligibility,
paper size, range placement or exits. It makes no provider requests. The fixed
registry admits prospective observations only from 2026-09-20 02:25 UTC onward;
its remote publication must precede that boundary before any promotion analysis
may use that label. The publication commit is retained in the PR history.

Every lane has four prespecified univariate feature rankings. Every configured
ranking is reported, including poor or missing results; none is selected after
seeing returns. These are candidate prioritization research hypotheses, not new
trading gates. Pump studies buyer breadth/growth, net flow and concentration.
Pons studies breadth/growth, concentration and quoted round-trip friction.
Meteora studies volume/fee acceleration, drift and pre-entry projected net return.
Ramses studies volume acceleration, two-way chop, flow imbalance and turnover.

Run `python -m certification.shadow --run-dir /retained/certification-sustained
--output /new/shadow-result` against the retained supervisor result and lane
telemetry journals. Hash-chain verification precedes extraction. Each score
retains its exact source event hash, policy hash and research registry hash.
The tool rejects source-policy mismatches and keeps all lane outputs separate.
Complete/fresh Pons vectors use the original five-second limit. Missing fields,
late evidence, screening-only observations and features first archived after
their outcome are excluded from prospective economic denominators.

No alternative range is studied in this revision. Only the actual original
prospectively frozen range can be referenced. A later range experiment must
register and persist every proposal from one authenticated pre-entry state before
any outcome; hindsight range selection is forbidden.

Forward outcomes remain unobserved until native adapters can authenticate exact
lifecycle/path/cost joins. In particular, Ramses scan rows are finalized screening
evidence, not receipt-authenticated qualification, and qualified Meteora features
first copied into a post-lifecycle checkpoint are not a prospective feature
record. Their native entry journals are the next required adapters. Zero missing
returns are imputed, and a forced machinery settlement is never an economic
strategy outcome. Full path MFE/MAE, fee/inventory decomposition and capital-hour
returns remain unavailable when the source does not prove them.

The statistical helper excludes all unjoined outcomes, reports exact tie-aware
win/loss AUC only after at least 30 outcomes spanning ten distinct assets, and
always refuses automatic promotion. Those are exploratory reporting bounds,
not a power calculation or proof of edge. Promotion requires a separately
registered later validation cohort and review of costs, censoring and dependence.

Validation: three research tests cover exact/missing feature values, policy and
time boundaries, censored/screening/post-outcome evidence, tiny samples and forced
outcomes. The existing 23 supervisor/control tests remain passing.

Next work: connect natural native entry/exit/mark records to these fixed
observations without generating extra provider work, then report which pre-entry
features discriminate cost-adjusted outcomes. No performance improvement is
claimed by this registration alone.
