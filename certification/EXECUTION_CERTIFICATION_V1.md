Purpose: prove that each Meme Machine lane can complete genuine natural PAPER lifecycles before using trade scarcity as evidence of profitability.

This revision is deliberately not a profitability claim. All four lanes remain read-only/paper-only with no signer, submission, allocation authority, or live-money capability. ## Certification interpretation

A trade under this revision proves machinery only. It must never be counted as evidence that the profitability-oriented policy would have traded or been profitable.

The next tightening stage should use completed natural lifecycle evidence to decide which relaxed gates discriminate outcomes. Thresholds must not be changed during an active observation window merely to manufacture additional trades.
