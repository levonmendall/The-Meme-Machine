# Reset-Recovery Research v1 — isolated preparation

Status: **RESEARCH PREP ONLY — NOT ACTIVE**

Parent: `53687bbbf107f8250bbf93b5533516586674479b`

This stages the research contract for a new directional regime:

**prior momentum thesis fails -> asset survives -> flush exhausts -> structure stabilizes -> fresh independent recovery demand appears**

It does **not** loosen Current or Survivor.

## Why this is separate

Current is designed to capture early momentum. Survivor is designed to capture later continuation/breakout after proven survival.

Reset-recovery asks a different question: can a structurally healthy asset produce a profitable recovery after a sharp flush even when continuation momentum temporarily failed?

If later promoted, it must have its own strategy/policy identity and its own profitability evidence.

## Initial research universe

Start with assets that have already reached the canonical post-graduation venue:

- Pump.fun -> canonical PumpSwap lineage;
- Pons -> canonical post-graduation market lineage.

Include:

- Current rejects;
- Survivor non-qualifiers;
- assets that were previously qualified but later lost momentum, provided no active-position conflict is created.

Pre-graduation reset entries are explicitly **not authorized** by this research contract.

## Research state machine

1. observed
2. flush candidate
3. stabilizing
4. recovery watch
5. shadow qualified
6. shadow invalidated
7. retired

No state grants allocation authority.

## Evidence that must exist

A useful reset candidate must be describable with point-in-time evidence for:

- prior high and flush path;
- retained liquidity and executable exit depth;
- concentration trajectory;
- creator/distribution safety;
- sell-flow deceleration;
- cessation of expanding new lows;
- fresh independent buyers;
- stabilization/base duration;
- recovery of VWAP or another frozen reference;
- breakout/recovery structure;
- turnover/depth and 1x/2x after-cost execution capacity;
- freshness/generation.

The research collector should use the same persisted/canonical evidence and Current->Survivor identity continuity already being prepared. It may not add provider work solely for research to a decision-critical path.

## No thresholds yet

This staging layer deliberately leaves all economic parameters unset, including:

- how deep the flush must be;
- how long stabilization must last;
- what liquidity retention is enough;
- what recovery reference to use;
- what demand return is sufficient;
- entry extension;
- stop;
- realization;
- trail;
- maximum hold;
- capital allocation.

Those values must come from an outcome-blind prospective research sample, then be frozen before an independent acceptance sample.

## Overlap controls

Reset-recovery research may observe an asset while Current or Survivor owns exposure, but it cannot trade.

If the regime is ever promoted, duplicate exposure rules must be explicit and enforced through the same shared family sleeve. A rejection by Current or Survivor never automatically qualifies Reset; it must prove its own thesis from fresh evidence.

## Required outcome analysis

For executable shadow entries preserve:

- after-cost return;
- MFE/MAE;
- 5m / 15m / 1h / 6h / 24h outcomes;
- time to recovery and invalidation;
- whether Current or Survivor later qualified;
- whether the event was really a staged-scaling opportunity instead of a distinct reset;
- capacity and the binding execution cap.

The goal is to prove **incremental alpha**, not merely find tokens that eventually bounced.

## Stage-E isolation

No Stage-E branch, candidate, workflow, diagnostic budget or workload is changed.

No allocation, market run, or live-money authority is introduced.
