## Approved mechanism and source protection

The committer invokes one admission rendezvous immediately before forming one new ordinary blocks request. Account, ACK, and control heads bypass. The coordinator evaluates returned hint generation/epoch/age, previous source and maintenance future completion, local backlog/age bounds, runtime lifecycle, and owner idle/empty/handoff state. Native debt, Needs, deadlines, reservations, side selection, and durable progress remain authoritative only inside the unchanged native runtime and MaintenanceArbiter.

An eligible opening consumes its hint epoch and establishes an absolute monotonic deadline at open + 0.100 seconds. An event wakes the existing maintenance coroutine immediately. Actual PriorityOwner.submit acceptance creates a future carrying its acceptance time and owner sequence; a coroutine wake cannot satisfy the offer. Equality with the deadline is expired. The owner checks expiry and idle/empty/handoff state under its existing condition lock, closing a competing urgent or older admission race.

Source resumes on acceptance or expiry, without awaiting maintenance completion. A separate acknowledgement barrier blocks another ordinary maintenance submission, including fast resubmission, until the affected source is actually submitted or aborted. Accepted futures survive waiter cancellation. The accepted barrier also survives an admitted-frame shutdown drain. No owner/database lock is held while the coroutine waits.

A consumed epoch cannot retry for a useful result. Planning, no demand, worker pending, yield, refusal, exception, and timeout all consume the attempt. Rearm requires completed affected source and a new eligible hint epoch. Overshoot is recorded without clamping and disables further offers.

At 16 or more pending frames the gate bypasses. The production batching function is called after rendezvous. Existing source wire order, 8-frame/16-MiB batching, reception/decode/dispatch bounds, source progress, urgent priority, checkpoint protocol, and accepted nonurgent FIFO remain intact. Ordinary submit calls omit the new optional deadline keyword; existing wrappers remain compatible.

The canonical charge implementation and workload inputs are unchanged. The finite placement model uses real native source_batch calls and charges exactly max(0, .165 * actual_frame_count - native_source_elapsed). Its source-only logical native interval is 0.020 seconds. Gate delay and maintenance service are separately accounted and provide no source-charge credit.
