## Query, replay and replacement economics

[Robinhood eth_getLogs](https://www.alchemy.com/docs/chains/robinhood-chain/robinhood-chain-api-endpoints/eth-get-logs) lists Free 10 blocks and PAYG unrestricted range, with 150 MB responses. The [support clarification](https://support.alchemy.com/articles/1883074130-why-do-i-see-a-free-tier-error-when-calling-eth-getlogs-with-a-large-block-range) describes a 10,000-block / 10,000-log alternative plus the general payload cap. Thus “unlimited” is not unbounded work or guaranteed completion. This application retains much smaller 2 MB transport, 1,000-page-log, 256 KB density, 16,384-event, 64-attempt and deadline limits. Ten-block defaults are an entitlement/proof fallback, not a statement that PAYG forbids larger ranges.

20/40/100/500-block arithmetic is in COST_MODEL.json; widths above 160 are not implemented or approved by the current capability schema. For 160 total blocks, width 10/20/40/100/160 needs 16/8/4/2/1 elements respectively. Only actual nonempty exact-filter parity, current canonical boundary, payload and original deadline evidence can select a larger width. Saturation/range failures add starts/CU before bounded subdivision; a larger rejected request can cost more than the original path.

The source retains a 6,000-slot replay planning bound. [Current historical replay documentation](https://www.alchemy.com/docs/reference/yellowstone-grpc-historical-replay) advertises 432,000 slots, whereas the existing engineering contract is narrower. Admin tools do not expose this team's accepted depth/filter/concurrency. Preserve 6,000 and archive fallback until authenticated larger replay supplies every scoped status/log/linked-finality obligation. A longer replay does not automatically eliminate missing WebSocket log bodies.

For EVM delivery, gross log-query replacement breaks even at **1,500 delivered bytes per eliminated 60-CU element** before any extra enrichment, fallback or infrastructure: `0.04 × bytes + 10 × subscribe_elements + 10 × unsubscribe_elements + extra_CU < 60 × eliminated_elements`. A one-element-per-three-second sensitivity allows 1.296 GB/month for zero extra work, saving at most $27.216 of log CU; three elements allows 3.888 GB/$81.648. Subscribe and unsubscribe cost 10 CU each; reconnects, extra headers and recovery consume additional headroom. These are byte thresholds, not observed traffic. The ordinary/high event rate is unknown. Native quotes and USD remain.

For Solana gRPC versus a 40-CU transaction body, the gross byte break-even is 280 KB at the public rates, before residual RPC/repair. Status-only gRPC plus joined logs may already be cheaper than full gRPC bodies. Removing content after delivery does not reduce billing. Account slicing only affects account data; it cannot strip transaction metadata fields required for native economic decoding.

## Plan decision

The [public Free tier](https://support.alchemy.com/articles/5827006872-what-is-included-in-the-free-tier) offers 30 M account CU. It could cover a limited HTTP-only subset, but a 212-CU three-second Pons owner uses 183.168 M core CU/30d before USD/discovery. An existing optimized Pump Survivor warm quote uses 20.736 M; additional Current concentration, shared discovery/streaming, USD, recovery and actual capacity can exceed the allowance. [Pricing](https://www.alchemy.com/pricing) lists gRPC differently from its [product page](https://www.alchemy.com/solana-grpc), which advertises no plan prerequisite. Do not downgrade or split apps/accounts based on that ambiguity. Free is not demonstrated sufficient for complete autonomous trading.

Enterprise or dedicated-node break-even requires a quote: `fixed commitment + discounted CU × workload + delivery + residual products + operations < current total`. Fixed support/capacity does not eliminate native reads, gaps or execution proof. There is no authenticated quote or sustained volume supporting either purchase. Public PAYG capacity is far above the local two-RPS governor; changing a local safety limit or buying capacity still requires actual entitlement and original deadline proof. [Account-wide throughput guidance](https://support.alchemy.com/articles/3788245542-is-alchemy-s-throughput-cu-s-limit-shared-across-all-apps-or-set-per-chain) says the ordinary allowance is shared across apps/chains; no independent Solana-plus-Robinhood budget is assumed.
