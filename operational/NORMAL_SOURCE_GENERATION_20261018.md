This component follows the reviewed prewrite core at
`8f3c364f81ae6389029f83d419edc57876aee9aa`. It permits the sealed Supervisor
to identify its actual new source child without reclassifying the retained
historical callbacks. It supplies no funding, market completeness, protection,

## Fixed acquisition and provenance

`normal_source_boot.SourceLaunch(BootContext)` accepts only the private current
BootContext. It issues at most one anonymous Unix STREAM channel in that
Supervisor process. The fixed evidence-worker entry accepts that inherited
descriptor through `--normal-boot-channel`; the channel closes before source
preparation workers start. No credential or provider endpoint crosses it.

Both peers verify the actual root SEALED record, its immutable original byte
hash, the original certificate and acceptance window, application commit/tree,
epoch/inception, boot and native clock, Supervisor PID/start/InvocationID, and
actual child PID/start. The child additionally verifies SO_PEERCRED, parentage,
the fixed systemd unit and its own installed source. The original certificate
window is never extended. Partial IPC reads retain their original absolute
deadline. A final entry check refuses a revoked or replaced
instance before provider/runtime constructors. The legacy OBSERVATION path and
its original financial guard remain unchanged.

The returned `NativeSourceGeneration` is process bound and privately issued;
serialized packet dictionaries are not capabilities. Its read-only
`identity_sha256`, `generation` and copied `identity()` expose provenance, not a
completion Boolean. `SelectiveSource.source_boot_identity_sha256` is an
optional, cached side channel for later genuine downstream publication. It is
`None` on the unchanged legacy path. Expensive source/systemd verification
occurs at acquisition/lifecycle boundaries, not for each market event.

## Original processing and retained history

The actual SelectiveSource uses its original queues, handlers, commit ordering,
session joins, FULL/ACK, cancellation and teardown. Its private run identity is
the new generation. The actual canonical writer owns publication under the
original transaction. The actual FinalizedFence provider identity is retained
alongside the root provenance.

Only `source_drain_current_generation` is new. Its bounded wrapper holds the
typed producer identity and the original source's actual running/terminal
census. The original four retained drain/recovery inputs remain byte-bound;
their two unknown callbacks are never acknowledged or relabeled. The original
historical incident pins and OBSERVATION recovery rules remain unchanged.
Publishing a current-generation terminal record does not require a still-open
root launch after the work has already been accepted; it still requires the
same actual process, SQLite owner, provider and original historical boundary.

Before a subsequent prewrite consumption, a present current-generation record
must have an original committed DRAINED census and no pending handlers. Its
producer is checked against the immutable protected root record, original
application/controller/epoch provenance and actual dead source PID/start.
Previous records are preserved by their existing content-addressed retention
mechanism before a new generation begins. A failed/unjoined generation remains
unresolved; this component cannot turn it into a successful empty restart.

The terminal record preserves the source's existing actual handler, queue,
session-preservation and pending history/proof counts. These are the original
bounded census, **not** a new exhaustive journal of every successful session or
economic event. A DRAINED source can still have pending history publications;
the genuine native fixture tests this explicitly. General semantic recovery
of a failed future generation, including any additional per-session lineage
needed for it, remains a separate finite capability. No old special incident
is generalized to arbitrary cancellation.
