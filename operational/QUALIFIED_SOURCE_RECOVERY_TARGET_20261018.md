# Qualified failed-source recovery target

This unselected app-only component binds the inputs of one failed, previously
qualified NEW source generation. It issues neither execution authority nor a
recovery receipt. Existing legacy incident selectors, old handler pins, source
startup refusal, provider limits, financial epoch and all four strategies are
unchanged. It performs no writes, providers, subscriptions or recovery actions.

`derive_qualified_recovery_target(StoppedNativeReadContext)` requires the exact
existing read-context class, its authenticated original full financial replay
and flat authority, live owner/deadline, original stopped/census/inode/transaction
guard, and complete registered-source population. It invokes the original
failed-generation extractor, including the fixed protected prior root-record
read and actual dead-producer check. It adds equality with the already-replayed
financial epoch/inception, actual configured endpoint identity and original
native clock identity. There is no new financial or native book constructor,
fallback database open, permission, root route or caller-supplied completion flag.

The immutable, unpicklable `QualifiedSourceRecoveryTarget` retains:

- Prior launch, producer, generation, failed-census and registry identities.
- Original session EOF/closure references and real retained handlers joined to
  their candidate gaps and CONTROL replay ranges.
- The union of every registered scope and every actual handler gap/control
  reference, preserving scout nomination-history uncertainty and NULL uppers.
- Original issued outbox/frontier metadata, explicitly without independent
  membership or native endpoint completion.
- Raw historical source-input hashes and the original two unknown handlers.
  The current CONTROL obligation is separate and never reclassified as an old
  handler disposition.

The original root seal authenticates application/controller/epoch provenance.
The child PID, endpoint, registry and census remain trusted native-writer facts;
they are not independently root-sealed complete economic assertions. Genuine
terminal accounting is distinct from a crash's unknown queue/handler tail. A
stopped stream or retained maximum slot is never an authenticated final upper.
Every target requires a fresh genuine upper, canonical replay, original consumer
FULL/ACK and proof publication, and separate downstream/native protection checks.
All recovery, historical completion, funding and normal-readiness flags stay
false. Failed raw state and callback completion are never relabeled DRAINED.

`verify_qualified_recovery_target(target)`, `.value()` and `.identity_sha256`
rejoin the underlying bounded registry/session EOF, closure/handler/gap/control
references, protected prior root record, endpoint/clock and original caller
guard before exposing the metadata. Foreign process/thread, guard loss, changed
inputs, expiry or context close refuses. The original 100,000-session/32MiB
encoded-input limits remain; the new union is limited to 100,000 obligations and
the returned encoded value to 32MiB. These are finite encoded-data limits, not a
Python RSS guarantee or a production capacity measurement.

## Executor handoff remains missing

The detached metadata is deliberately tethered to its original short read
context. It cannot cross that context's expiry/close into the existing 120-second
economic recovery provider/writer path. No execution-request issuer, detached
stopped/apply-fence capability or separate authenticated recovery receipt is
implemented here. Current prewrite refusal remains unchanged.

The next independently reviewed bridge must be issued by the actual protected
`native-recover` request inside its original stopped/apply fences. It must retain
those locks and the caller's original absolute clock, close short SQL snapshots
before network I/O or writes, and rejoin fresh original readonly snapshots per
bounded work step. It must not keep a 10-second SQL reader open across provider
work, renew the recovery deadline, broaden legacy pins, or weaken the original
flat epoch gate and 300/65-second outer controls. Held-position continuation is
a separate required protocol. Genuine recovery may discharge only its explicit
economic scope while leaving old unbounded/scout history and callback failure
facts unchanged.

## Final-work lifetime correction

Independent review of `4d5a6ec4` found that final target hashing and detached
copying could cross the original scope expiry or lose its guard after the
underlying population checks. The successor now calls the existing original
`verify_boundary()` after the verification digest, after the final detached
copy, and after initial construction's own copy/hash before returning a target.
It neither renews a deadline nor repeats the population scan for these checks.

produce five expected failures: deadline and guard loss during final hashing,
deadline and guard loss during final copying, and expiry during initial mint's
final hash. They use the real read context and actual private failed source
fixture; only the exact final work boundary is gated. The counterfactual log is
`qualified-source-recovery-target-final-work-4d5a-counterfactual.log`, with
retained scratch `/tmp/mm-engineering-5g1v15qb`. The successor result is
`qualified-source-recovery-target-final-work-focused.log`. The earlier 8+24
