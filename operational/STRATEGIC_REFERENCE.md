## RESEARCH_ONLY — rejected Pump winner hypothesis

The historical hypothesis selects concentration rejections with at least 25 evidence events and independent-group fraction at least 0.02. A clean winner required MFE at least 15% and MAE strictly above -10%. Of 120 complete rejected outcomes there were eight clean winners; 61 concentration rejections contained four. The matching 15-candidate subset contained four winners (26.67%, approximately 4.07x enrichment), but **73.33% fell below -10%**. Downside was unacceptable: automatic trading admission and trading-rule freeze were explicitly forbidden.

Prospective validation requires at least 30 matching candidates and 30 concentration controls, no refit, at least 2x winner-rate lift, at least 15% matching clean winners, median MAE above -10% and at most 50% below -10%. This protocol is preserved for research, not activated.

Do not activate that hypothesis. Pump reset thresholds were not frozen: useful repeated evidence disappeared outside microcap dependence.

This is a provisional **shadow** trigger, distinct from approved >=2x staged-winner scaling. Add amount and total exposure were not established by this public-price research.

This is a provisional shadow shape, requiring executable liquidity, safe concentration and creator/distribution state, selling exhaustion, independent demand recovery, recovery-reference proof, turnover/depth, 1x/2x execution stress and freshness/generation. Public chart evidence cannot establish execution costs, buyer breadth or after-cost profitability and grants no PAPER allocation.
