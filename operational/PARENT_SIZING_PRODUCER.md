# Read-only parent sizing component

`parent_sizing.ParentSizingProducer` takes the exact original Pump
ReadOnlyFailoverRPC and Pons PacedRpc with exact owned ChildRPCPorts. The protected
parent constructs them against its existing metered source aliases and disposable
private immutable caches. The producer creates no provider, credential, financial
book, ledger, grant or epoch. Missing ports fail; there is no URL or zero-capital
fallback. Its scope is read-only qualification sizing data for contained runners.

The administration caller must continuously hold its original
`stopped_snapshot_fence()` around its real `_financial(frozen=True)` full replay
and the contained run. No app helper can prove that host fence from supplied JSON.
`financial_basis` checks the exact full replay result/state binding, journal and
combined sequences, epoch/inception, projection hash, original capital view and
all nine original obligation counts. It requires genuine flatness, including
legacy native pending obligations. Recomputed hashes are integrity checks and
are explicitly not authentication. The protected integration must retain its
existing installed-source and policy/configuration approvals and authenticate
the exact resulting sizing receipt on its private child channel. This component
does not implement that administrative action or privileged execution path.

Pump first validates the genuine source-bound getGenesisHash against mainnet,
then uses the original finalized getMultipleAccounts SOL oracle request and
`usd_valuation.sol_usd` decoder, with the hash of its actual response. Pons uses
the original persistent NativeValueReader with the supplied native metered RPC:
real chain validation, pinned oracle/interface/token/deployment authentication,
executable native-to-USDG route and final ordered canonical block fences.
Physical requests and batches remain the original parent Budget reservations,
HTTPReceipt delivery/EOF, Governor/Admission and purchase accounting. Logical
cache consumers stay visible. No independent signing, funding or trading exists.

Each lane's realized USD equity is the exact production
`_size_equity(state,lane+'_current')` used by SharedNativePortfolio.equity. Both
shared_realized_equity and effective_family_equivalence use their genuine full
replay inputs, including family_sizing_genesis and realized regime history. The
original capital_view supplies free cash. Exact rational division by the original
USDValue.amount(1,now) computes the integer native-unit floor, independently of
ambient Decimal precision. Proven all-bucket flatness supplies reserved zero.
Unknown obligations or non-flat state cannot take this path.

The receipt follows isolated-native-sizing-v1 exactly, with native lamports/wei,
current source's decision policies, epoch reference and combined portfolio
sequence. Underlying USD evidence hashes and observed/valid-until times are
unchanged. The receipt expires at the earlier underlying expiry, at most the
original Pons five-second quote, potentially sooner if SOL was already old.
Compatible original quotes can be reused, never extended or relabeled fresh.
The protected owner must refresh before expiry; native consumers reject stale
facts. Acquisition failure produces no receipt or alternative positive basis.

ProducedSizingReceipt is detached immutable data with separate provenance for
the original policy and financial contract hashes, replay head and obligation
counts. It deliberately is not AuthenticatedSizingFacts, a host fence, financial
authority or a readiness result. Authentication/fence booleans are false in its
provenance because those facts can only be established by the protected caller.

Tests use actual temporary $500 migration and $1,000 new-epoch journals, original
read-only full replay and original sizing/USD decoders. Public provider connect
is faked behind real source-bound IPC, original metering/admission, private native
caches and complete native HTTP batches. Cases prove exact floors/both policies,
ambient precision independence, old SOL and five-second Pons expiries, detached
data, unresolved obligations and bad head rejection, no extra compatible quote
purchase, missing executable route and wrong-chain refusal. No production file,
external provider, AWS workflow, production financial write or new privilege is involved.
Initial fixture failures remain at /tmp/mm-engineering-rrxvy6ze (missing reporting
reader path, duplicate test pacer argument); those fixtures were corrected.
The final eight focused cases passed in 8.939 seconds. Nonnegative original
sequences remain accepted as the source replay and isolated receipt contracts do.
